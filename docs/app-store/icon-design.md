The icon is original generated artwork created with the built-in image-generation tool on 11 November 2026, then resized to an opaque 1024 x 1024 PNG for the Xcode app-icon asset catalog. No third-party logo or learner recording is used. Apple applies the platform icon mask.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final iOS app icon, square 1024 by 1024 pixels. Create an elegant, highly legible icon for 'Cello', a calm beginner music practice app. Full-bleed deep forest green background matching RGB 41,77,66, with a single large centered warm ivory and muted amber-gold cello emblem. The cello must unmistakably have a long slender neck, small scroll, symmetrical curved shoulders, pinched waist with C-bouts, rounded lower bout, four fine strings, a simple bridge and slim endpin. Use a carefully balanced, refined flat graphic silhouette with restrained dimensional warmth, crisp clean edges, generous safe margins, and strong recognition at tiny home-screen sizes. The cello is upright and fills roughly 75 percent of the square's height. No letters, no words, no musical notes, no extra objects, no border, no rounded-square outline, no watermark. Opaque background all the way to every edge; Apple applies the icon mask. Render the icon artwork itself, no phone or presentation mockup.
