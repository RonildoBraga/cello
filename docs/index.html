Help & support

Small lessons in listening, humming and rhythm, with a path towards playing the cello.

Start practising

Open Music and begin with First sounds. Hear an example, then try a gentle hum or a rhythm lesson. Every lesson stays available to practise again.

Scores give feedback on the sound and timing the app can measure.

Microphone and camera

The microphone records your practice and measures pitch. Humming records sound only. Cello recording also uses the camera to capture your hands and bow.

You can change permissions in iPhone Settings. Listening to examples needs neither permission.

Your recordings

Find saved takes in Recordings. Export a take when you want to keep a copy or share it for feedback.

Delete individual takes there, or choose Edit → Delete all. Earned lesson progress is kept when recordings are deleted. Export important takes before deleting the app.
