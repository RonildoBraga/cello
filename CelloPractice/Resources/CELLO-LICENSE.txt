These are excerpts of recorded bowed cello-section sustains with vibrato from the susvib collection. They are not synthesized oscillators or new recordings of a solo player. The source does not establish which physical string/fingering was used, so these are pitch references rather than demonstrations of open-string technique.

Changes for Cello:
- Kept source seconds 0.15–3.35, producing a 3.2-second excerpt.
- Downmixed stereo 44,100 Hz / 24-bit PCM to mono 48,000 Hz / signed 16-bit PCM.
- Applied a 10 ms raised-cosine fade in and 50 ms raised-cosine fade out.
- Applied a constant gain per excerpt so the maximum absolute sample is approximately 0.65.
- No pitch correction or synthetic partials were added to these bundled source excerpts. Natural bow noise and small pitch variation remain. The app renderer may make a small constant playback-rate adjustment to match its explicit equal-tempered target.

Source note labels use an octave number one below scientific pitch notation: source C1/G1/D2/A2 are audibly C2/G2/D3/A3. The measured frequencies below were checked independently with the app's PitchDetector; the target was not passed to the detector. Measurements use 8,192-frame windows at 48 kHz, a 4,410-frame hop, and confidence >= 0.75, with window starts from 0.4 seconds through the available sustained excerpt.
