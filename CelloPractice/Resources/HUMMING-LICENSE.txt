Melody adaptations (recorded-human references)

The melody examples use the same human recording, edited into the traditional Hot Cross Buns teaching arrangement. Pitch, note duration, articulation and quiet gaps are adjusted to the selected score. They are adaptations of a recorded voice, not a continuous performance of this melody by the original creator. No endorsement by the original creator is implied.
